    How the Recovery Score works. Each morning it blends three signals: last night's HRV against your own average (40%), deep sleep minutes against a 90 minute target (35%), and yesterday's average stress (25%). If one signal is missing, the others are re-weighted. Bands: 75+ Prime, 55 to 74 Ready, 40 to 54 Moderate, below 40 Recover.
